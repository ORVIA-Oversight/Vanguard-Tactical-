# Vanguard Tactical — Brand System 2026

## Core brand position

Vanguard Tactical is a human-led operational platform for command, tracking, communications, readiness and field coordination.

The parent proposition is:

**See the operation. Command the response.**

Airsoft and paintball are secondary Sport & Recreation use cases beneath the wider operational platform.

---

## Brand hierarchy

### Full Vanguard mark
Use for:
- corporate identity
- website header/footer
- parent-platform pages
- commercial materials
- company-level sponsorship
- major presentations
- master favicon/app family

Meaning:
The complete mark brings together command, people, training, progression, community and real-world capability.

### Excalibur / sword submark
Use for:
- Command
- ATAC / field overview
- operational picture
- control-room views
- decisive actions
- maps, tasking and command dashboards

Meaning:
Purpose, judgement and earned responsibility. Authority must be used deliberately.

### Gladiator / helmet submark
Use for:
- Teams
- leadership
- training
- resilience
- 6 Troop / 7 Troop
- team apparel and equipment
- team command suite

Meaning:
Controlled strength, resilience, preparation and composure under pressure.

### Road + lone figure / place of peace submark
Use for:
- participant / personal workspace
- welfare
- progression
- development pathways
- community
- onboarding
- learning and after-action reflection

Meaning:
The road is progression. The figure is the human destination: capability should create the space to live, belong and enjoy what comes next.

---

## Brand story

The mark is not intended to glorify aggression.

The symbolism is:

1. **Excalibur** — leadership and responsibility must be earned.
2. **The Gladiator** — disciplined strength, resilience and preparation.
3. **The Road** — growth through training, repetition, teamwork and responsibility.
4. **The Lone Figure** — the human outcome: move through challenge and reach a place of confidence, community and peace.

Working brand line:

**Prepare seriously. Act responsibly. Build people. Create the space to enjoy what comes next.**

Core values line:

**People | Training | Community | Real-world skills**

## Airsoft programme

Airsoft remains a secondary Vanguard sector.

Vanguard Tactical sponsors:

### 6 Troop
Primary sponsored airsoft team.

### 7 Troop
Reserve and augmentation element supporting the main team.

The programme includes:
- Team Leader
- 2IC
- defined team functions
- personalised player workspace
- attendance and event planning
- equipment readiness
- shared team kit
- training and development
- ATAC field layer where enabled
- after-action review
- sponsorship and commercial partnerships
- reinvestment into team kit, event costs and growth

The structure borrows useful organisational ideas from UK military team structures for clarity and accountability. It does **not** imply military affiliation or qualification.

The team is intentionally mixed:
- experienced players
- former service personnel
- established airsofters
- developing players
- complete novices

The common standard is:
- reliability
- safe play
- respect
- teamwork
- contribution
- willingness to learn

## Usage rule

Do not invent separate logos for each product.

Use the submarks as **signposting symbols within one Vanguard brand**.

Full mark = Vanguard
Sword = Command / ATAC
Gladiator = Teams
Road / figure = People / progression

This keeps the estate recognisable while giving each operational area its own visual identity.
